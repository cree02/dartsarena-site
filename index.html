Features

Everything a tournament night needs

Built by darts players for leagues, pubs, clubs and the garage board with friends.

Brackets that run themselves

Enter your players and DartsArena draws a fair double-elimination bracket. You get to play darts instead of doing paperwork.

  • Byes placed automatically, for any number of players.
  • Losers drop into a consolation bracket, so everyone plays at least twice.
  • Matches go straight to the next free board, and winners advance as soon as a result is confirmed.
  • Fix a name, override a result or move a match at any time.

Tap where the dart lands

Score 501, 301 or Cricket, as a single leg or best of 3 or 5. Tap each dart on the board, or type the three-dart total.

  • Remaining scores, legs and three-dart averages update as you go.
  • High scores, 180s and checkout percentages are tracked for you.
  • Optional double-out, and undo for any turn.

Every TV becomes a scoreboard

Open the address shown in the app on any smart TV, laptop or tablet on the same Wi-Fi. There's nothing to install on the TV.

  • A venue dashboard shows every live board and who's up next.
  • A lane display for each board shows big, readable scores, whose turn it is and a match clock.

Score from any phone

Hand out the scoring. Other players open DartsArena, pick your tournament from the list and enter a 4-digit code.

  • Each board can be scored from its own phone.
  • The bracket updates on every device and TV straight away.
  • Scorers don't need to buy anything.

How it works

From sign-up sheet to final in three steps

Add players and boards

Type in names, choose 501, 301 or Cricket, and set how many boards you have.

Put the scores on the TVs

Open the address from the app in any browser on your Wi-Fi. Phones can join as scorers with a code.

Throw darts

DartsArena calls the matches, assigns boards, and moves everyone through the bracket to a champion.
